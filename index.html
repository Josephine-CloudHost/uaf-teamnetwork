App Guide & Purpose
Overview of tabs and operational workflows
›

UAF Team Network is the unified communication, management, and resource platform for Upskill Africa Foundation staff and volunteers.

  • Main Feed: Real-time organizational updates, announcements, and opportunity digests.
  • Contacts: Staff directory with roles, positions, and contact channels.
  • Partnerships: Centralized register of strategic, implementing, and funding partners.
  • Documents: Safe repository for policies, reports, toolkits, and project files.
  • Tasks: Activity assignment, timeline tracking, and reminder sweeps.
  • UAF Finances: Multi-currency (USD & LRD) budget tracking, grant accounting, initiative ledgers, and audited statements.
⚖️
Staff Code of Conduct & Ethics
NGO standard behavioral expectations
›

All members of UAF are expected to adhere to the highest standards of integrity, respect, and professionalism:

  1. Respect & Inclusion: Maintain zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or abusive conduct based on gender, ethnicity, religion, or background.
  2. Integrity in Operations: Accurately record financial entries, project metrics, and administrative activities without falsification.
  3. Safeguarding: Actively protect the rights, privacy, and safety of youth, beneficiaries, and community partners.
🛡️
Confidentiality & Data Protection Policy
Non-misuse policy referencing applicable law
›

All data, documents, beneficiary lists, and financial records within this platform are proprietary and strictly confidential under applicable data protection legislation and organizational agreements:

  • Non-Disclosure: Proprietary data may not be copied, distributed, or shared with third parties without written authorization from the Executive Director.
  • Account Security: Session credentials and passwords must remain confidential. Account sharing is strictly prohibited.
🚪
Resignation & Offboarding Procedure
Standard protocol upon separation from UAF
›

Upon formal resignation or conclusion of service with UAF:

  1. Complete standard handovers of all assigned documents, project assets, and pending tasks.
  2. Digital access credentials and authorized roles will be retired automatically upon separation.
  3. Non-disclosure commitments regarding organizational records continue to apply post-employment under law.
