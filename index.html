History of Upskill Africa Foundation
Origins, founders, and organizational growth
›

Founded July 2, 2023 by Chris Nen Torbor and Litrice M. Browne, when they reached out to Jeremiah Kollie and Michael Donor at their residence in Pipeline and presented the dream to educate Liberians and Africans.

The vision was embraced and supported especially by Michael B. Donor, operating over a full year with three core individuals: Torbor, Donor, and Browne. Originally operating informally as "Upskill - Education and Social Services".

In early 2024, Rejoice Korvah was recruited as Social Affairs Officer. Later in mid-2024, UAF recruited Michael Togbah (General Secretary), Gifty Promise Nimenee (Social Affairs Officer), Garmai M. Kollie (Chief Financial Officer), and Emmanuel Suah (Help Desk), implementing multiple programs. Subsequent recruits included Fatu Yamah and Moses Saah.

In 2026, UAF expanded its executive team: Stephen T. Powo, Jr. (Education Empowerment Officer), Frances Joyce Mawolo (Child Protection Officer), Joyce V. Cole (Resource Mobilization Officer), John Muyuneh (Financial Assistant), and Francilia F. Nagbe (Executive Assistant), alongside county coordinators in Maryland (Solomon Weah, Romeo S. Prowd, Ramble Romeo) and Nimba (Christopher Yelebo).

Upskill was formally registered on September 6, 2025 as Upskill Africa Foundation (UAF), partnered with Litrich Agro and Innovation Enterprise (IE).
