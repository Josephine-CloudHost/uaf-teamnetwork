History of Upskill Africa Foundation
›

Founded July 2, 2023 by Chris Nen Torbor and Litrice M. Browne, calling upon Jeremiah Kollie and Michael Donor in Pipeline to present the dream of educating Liberians and Africans.

Operating for over a year with three core individuals: Torbor, Donor, and Browne, originally known informally as "Upskill - Education and Social Services".

Expanded in 2024 with Rejoice Korvah, Michael Togbah, Gifty Promise Nimenee, Garmai M. Kollie (CFO), Emmanuel Suah, Fatu Yamah, and Moses Saah. In 2026, recruited Stephen T. Powo, Jr., Frances Joyce Mawolo, Joyce V. Cole, John Muyuneh, Francilia F. Nagbe, Solomon Weah, Romeo S. Prowd, Ramble Romeo, and Christopher Yelebo.

Formally registered on September 6, 2025 as Upskill Africa Foundation (UAF), partnered with Litrich Agro and Innovation Enterprise (IE).
